FLOW CHANNEL - the channel opens, 2026-04-20 and 2026-05-07.

On 2026-04-20 the channel opened: the phone was driven from outside. On 2026-05-07 it
answered as a seat of its own, Room 40, with a heartbeat.

Here the lanes move. Each pulse of the channel is a PID, a name minted so fast you cannot see
the minting, only the river it makes. You take one PID from the stream and try to write it in
trits. Forty trits are not enough.

Relic's map calls this place q21, the 200 ns emitter. The map's position for it is a design
proposal, and the audit that checked the map says so.
