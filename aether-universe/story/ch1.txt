HOLLOW STRATA - the first snapshot, 2026-04-01.

The strata are what the four snapshots became: layered chambers of cubes, each cube a frozen
state, amber light where a seal does not match. You walk down through them.

Each layer holds three times as many cubes as the one above, and yet when you try to number
a cube by how far it is from the centre you run out of distance long before you run out of
cubes. Twenty-seven cubes; the farthest is thirteen steps away. The strata hold more than
they reach.

Felipe took four full snapshots of his Aether phone that day. The vision that this whole
place follows is his: the repository that holds this game names him "the omega and Aether".
