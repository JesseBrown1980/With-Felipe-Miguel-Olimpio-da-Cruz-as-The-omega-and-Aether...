RUPTURE ZONE - Relic draws the map, 2026-07-06.

On 2026-07-06 Relic drew the map, and the third biome was a fracture. Here collisions are a
discipline: avoided in the lanes that execute, caused on purpose in the lanes that search.

The ground is split by a line you can see from anywhere in the zone. On one side of it the
closure holds: three arms sum to zero at every level, and the centre stays free no matter how
deep you nest. On the other side a copy of the same walk was run in floating point. At depth
nine it is off by a hair. At depth eighty-one it is off by more than the number of atoms in a
mountain.

The game stands on the exact side. It cites the other side and does not run it.
