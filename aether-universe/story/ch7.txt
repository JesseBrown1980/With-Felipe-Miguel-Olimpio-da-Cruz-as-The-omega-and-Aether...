27-CUBE CHAMBER - the laws are sealed, 2026-07-21.

The laws were sealed on 2026-07-21, twenty-seven of them, numbered 0 to 26 so the ledger
closes on itself. The chamber is a cube of twenty-seven cells. Stand at the centre: it is
empty.

Walk the nine cells along the centre planes: empty, except one small prime sitting in the room
marked three. The other eighteen cells are full, and nearly equally full. Everything that is
prime and greater than three lives in a cell that shares no factor with twenty-seven.
