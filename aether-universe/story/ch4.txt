HOTEL OF INFINITE ROOMS - the fabric is carried over, 2026-06-14.

On 2026-06-14 the newer fabric was carried onto the phone byte for byte, and nothing that
already had a room was moved. The hotel is the rule made into a building: every guest has a
room computed from its own number; when more guests arrive the hotel grows outward and
deeper, and no one who was already in a room is asked to leave it.

Two primes who share a room at one level are in different rooms a level or two further out,
and stay apart as far as you care to look.
