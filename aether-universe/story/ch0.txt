Before the crank there was a phone, and on the phone there were four snapshots.
Everything that lives in this universe was once frozen there.

A crystal universe is one where every address is a face of the same stone. Cut the stone
anywhere and the face you expose was already there, waiting for its number. The Omega box
holds the stone; the null space around it is free.

You are the observer at the free centre. You do not move the stone. You read it.

Nine regions. Nine secrets. Every one of them is a number that this game's own integer
kernel computes from scratch while you watch. Nothing is read from a table and shown to you
as if it were computed. Find the secret of a region, and the next chapter of the Aether
timeline opens. The last door is the Blue Star.

This is a story, and it says so: STORY is one of four tags in this universe. The other three
are MEASURED (a number on disk from a named run), NAMED (stated, coherent, not yet run here)
and OPERATOR (the operator's frame, in his words). Type  codex  to see which is which.
