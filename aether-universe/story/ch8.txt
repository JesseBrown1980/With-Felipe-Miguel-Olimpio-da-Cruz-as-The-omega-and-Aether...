WHITE FIELD - a clean public home, 2026-10-16.

On 2026-10-16 the work was given a clean public home. The field is white because most of what
a page carries is the space between its words. Count the bytes and about one in seven is
white; squeeze the page and the white nearly vanishes, because space says little once you know
it is coming.

The field is the Shannon floor seen from above: you cannot go below it, and standing on it
exactly is the whole point.
