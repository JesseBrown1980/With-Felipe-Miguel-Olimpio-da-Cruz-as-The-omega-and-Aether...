## 5. THE LADDER

> "Sha hash 256 to 3 to 1024 to 6 4096 to 9 ect"

| bits | index | as power of 4 |
|---:|---:|---|
| 256 | 3 | 4⁴ |
| 1024 | 6 | 4⁵ |
| 4096 | 9 | 4⁶ |
| 16384 | 12 | 4⁷ |
| 65536 | 15 | 4⁸ |

The rule: **width ×4, index +3.** Closed form `index = 3·(log₄ width − 3)`.
Width quadruples while the index steps by three — 3, 6, 9, 12, 15. This is the
same 3-6-9 spine as the existing 3 / 27 / 2187 / 6561 ladder and should be built
as one ladder, not two.
