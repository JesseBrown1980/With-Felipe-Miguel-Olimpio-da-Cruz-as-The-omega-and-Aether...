[1] THE GENUS LAW  — spheres merged along a chain
    place a sphere at every node, a tube along every calling.
    chi = 2V - 2E   and   chi = 2 - 2g   =>   g = E - V + 1
    so the GENUS of the merged object and the CYCLE RANK of the
    chain graph are the same integer. one theorem, both halves.

    verifying the law against surfaces whose genus is known:

[3] THE DENSITY GRADIENT — where I was wrong
    rho(M) = M / ((4/3) pi r_s^3)  with  r_s = 2GM/c^2
           = 3 c^6 / (32 pi G^3 M^2)
    the mass cancels once and returns squared in the denominator.
    SO AVERAGE DENSITY FALLS AS 1/M^2. bigger means LESS dense.
    he said "one that's not so densely gradient" is the chained,
    more massive one. that direction is correct and I dismissed it.
