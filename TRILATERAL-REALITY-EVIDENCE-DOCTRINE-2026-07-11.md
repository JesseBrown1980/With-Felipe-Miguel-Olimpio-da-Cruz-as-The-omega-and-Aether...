## No-deflate law

A model or agent must not erase a measured result because:

- it cannot see another device or drive;
- the public mirror excludes private corpus or keys;
- a newer migration is staged;
- deterministic gates coexist with neural models;
- storage rather than VRAM holds durable state;
- a classical result is not a physical quantum claim;
- the mechanism is represented by hashes/addresses plus a retained or distributed body;
- a stale document predates a newer measured implementation.

Use the correct tag and evidence route.

## No-inflate law

A model or agent must not claim:

- all logical addresses are resident agents;
- all packet tallies are model calls;
- disk performs neural tensor arithmetic;
- selectors contain missing payload entropy;
- classical Rust performs physical quantum cloning;
- ideal fidelity is zero physical error;
- a third-seat verification authorizes a live cutover or hardware fire.
